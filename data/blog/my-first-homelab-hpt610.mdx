There's something uniquely satisfying about breathing new life into old hardware. I was doom-scrolling on Carousell one day to find cheap or free items people wanted to let go. Then, I stumbled upon an old office computer for **just RM 60** (about USD ~13 converted), quite cheap! I was looking for few candidates previously but didn't fit my budget, so I think this time is a good oppurtunity to grab one. I bought one.

I liked the form factor that **wouldn't take up much space**, and the specs were quite OK for a beginner person (like me) who just **started on homelabbing**.

The model I've gotten is an **HP T610**. Rocking an **AMD T56N** (dual core) processor, comes with 4 GB RAM and 16 GB flash disk. I've upgraded some of the components. I figure the RAM and storage were quite small and limited, which leads to some upgrades!

This post documents my journey of transforming this modest machine into a capable home server, complete with hardware upgrades, software configuration, and the occasional troubleshooting adventure. If you're considering starting your own homelab, I hope my experience provides both inspiration and practical guidance.

I've purchased a **120 GB SSD** and a **4 GB RAM** stick on Shopee. The plan is to make the 120 drive as boot/main drive. This drive will store Proxmox host and VM(s) drive. I also have a **500 GB HDD** unused from an old laptop that I'll repurposed here as a storage drive.

This machine had one extra **SATA slot** so I add another hard disk I've taken from an unused old laptop I have. It has **500 GB of storage** and I plan to use it as **storage drive**, ie to store data like movies, backups, large files etc.

I designed and 3D-printed a _stopper thingy_ (the purple stuff) to prevent the hard disk from moving and possibly disconnects from its port. This idea was inspired from other peoples from [this forum](https://www.parkytowers.me.uk/thin/hp/t610/mods.shtml).
